Positive Duty

Role: Web Developer - UI/UX Designer
Company: JPL Media - Positive Duty -

Frontend-focused full-stack role on Positive Duty v2, a web platform delivering compliance training under Australia’s Respect at Work legislation. Contributed across the product lifecycle, with a strong focus on UI architecture, UX flows, and role-based platforms including employee, admin, and superadmin portals for multi-tenant deployments.

Frontend & UI/UX

  • Next.js, React, TypeScript, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS.
  • Responsive design, accessibility, and component-based UI architecture.
  • Wireframing and prototyping with Figma.

Product & Platform Features

  • Video-based training modules, certifications, progress tracking, reporting dashboards, and resource management interfaces.
  • Superadmin tools for multi-tenant configuration, content visibility, and client-level controls.

Authentication & Security

  • Custom authentication flows using Keycloak (OIDC, email OTP), session handling, and role-based access control.

Collaboration & Deployment

  • Close collaboration with backend and DevOps teams to ensure smooth integration and deployment using Kubernetes, Helm, and Rancher.
